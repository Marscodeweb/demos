About Vintage Point

Vintage Point is an antique and gift shop at 1450 25th St S in Fargo.

Vintage pieces carry history: the dishes someone's grandmother used, the sign from a store long closed, the lamp that lit a living room for decades.

Vintage Point gathers those treasures alongside fresh gifts and décor, so every visit turns up something new.
