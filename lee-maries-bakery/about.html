About Lee Marie's

Lee Marie's is a bakery at 13 8th St S in downtown Fargo.

There's something about walking into a bakery in the morning: the smell of butter and cinnamon, trays fresh from the oven.

Lee Marie's brings that feeling to downtown Fargo with pastries, breads and treats baked from scratch.
