Warning signs

When to call a plumber

Any of these can turn into water damage if they're ignored.

  • Water stain spreading on the ceiling
  • Toilet that runs all night
  • Drains gurgling or slow
  • Lukewarm showers
  • Rust-colored water
  • Damp cabinet under the sink
