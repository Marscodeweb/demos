About the maker

Kingdom Woodworking is a small Moorhead shop that builds furniture and woodwork the old-fashioned way: solid lumber, real joinery and finishes applied by hand.

Every commission is a conversation. We'll talk about how the piece will be used, where it will live and what it should feel like, then build it to last for decades.

– Kingdom Woodworking
