About Kerncrete

Kerncrete is a Fargo concrete contractor pouring and repairing residential flatwork.

Built for frost and thaw

Concrete in Fargo has to survive brutal freeze-thaw cycles. Most cracking and sinking comes down to what's underneath: the base, the grading and the reinforcement.

Kerncrete focuses on that groundwork so the surface you see stays flat and solid for years.
