The CleanEdge process

From sketch to finished bed

Most residential projects wrap up in a day or two once materials are in.

  1. Walk the yard together

    We visit, look at sun, slope and drainage, and listen to what you want from the space.

  2. Review a simple plan

    You get a layout and a fixed price for materials and labor before anything is dug.

  3. Install and clean up

    Our crew builds it, rakes everything smooth and hauls away the waste.
