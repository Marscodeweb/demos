About The Sudsy Pup

The Sudsy Pup Grooming is a Fargo dog grooming business.

Grooming should be something dogs look forward to. At The Sudsy Pup, every bath comes with patience, praise and a few treats.

From quick nail trims to full teddy-bear cuts, each pup gets one-on-one care and goes home looking and smelling great.
