About Chad Pikula Drywall

Chad Pikula Drywall LLC is a licensed drywall contractor based in West Fargo.

Drywall is the whole focus

Good drywall is invisible. You only notice it when seams show through paint or corners wave under the light.

Chad Pikula Drywall puts in the extra coats and sanding so walls look clean in any light, from basement rec rooms to vaulted ceilings.

How the work is done

  • Licensed. Active ND contractor license
  • Patient finishing. Coats given time to dry
  • Clean sites. Dust contained and swept
  • Clear pricing. Written quotes

Drywall work in: West Fargo, Fargo, Moorhead, Horace, Dilworth and Harwood. West Fargo and the surrounding metro.
