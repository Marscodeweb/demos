Pet parent questions

Before your first groom

Do you groom cats too?

Yes. Cats get calm, patient handling and as many breaks as they need.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well every 4 to 8 weeks, depending on coat type. JJ can recommend a schedule.

What if my pet is nervous?

Let JJ know when you book. Nervous pets get extra time and a slower pace.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just your pet on a leash or in a carrier, plus any special shampoo if your vet recommends one.
