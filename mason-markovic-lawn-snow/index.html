Good to know

Questions people ask Mason

Do I have to sign up for the whole season?

No. You can do a season plan or call for single visits. Mason will explain both when he quotes.

Can I do lawn only, or snow only?

Yes. Plenty of customers only want one. Pick whatever you need.

How soon after a snowfall do you come?

Route customers are cleared after each snowfall, in route order. Mason can tell you where your street falls.

Do you work outside Fargo?

West Fargo, Moorhead and the towns nearby are usually fine. Ask about your address.

Beat the first snowfall.

Routes fill up once the forecast turns. Reach out this week and lock in your spot for the winter.
