About Mills Lounge

Mills Lounge is a neighborhood bar at 6 Center Ave E in Dilworth.

Every town needs a place where people can unwind, see friends and catch the game. In Dilworth, that's Mills Lounge.

Cold drinks, simple bar food and a welcoming crowd make it easy to stop in for one and stay for a few.
